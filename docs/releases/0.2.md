Helm is now an agentic file manager. Ask it to organize, move and find your files. Nothing changes until you press Apply.

- **AI mode**, off until you turn it on. The trefoil at the top right opens the AI panel beside your files, with three suggestions for the folder on show.
- **Plan, then Apply.** Every change shows first, in groups you can untick. Moving to the Trash starts unticked. One Undo puts everything back, except copies and new folders where there is no Trash.
- **This Folder or Whole Mac.** A switch in the panel lets AI mode look through your home folder, cloud folders and disks, and propose moving files to where they belong. Never your Library, hidden files or system folders.
- **Read contents**, off until you turn it on. Helm reads inside text files, PDFs, pictures and videos on your Mac, and gives the model short summaries, never the files.
- **Find with AI.** Describe a file in the search field. The model plans the searches, and Helm runs them on your Mac. A row under the header keeps a search to one kind, and Read contents finds photos by what they show and the text in them.
- **Setup, step by step.** One window: Welcome, Full Disk Access, Default File Viewer, AI Mode, You're All Set. It carries on where it was after macOS quits and reopens Helm.
- **Your choice of model.** OpenAI with your ChatGPT plan or a key; Claude, Gemini and Grok with your own key; Ollama and LM Studio on your Mac, or any OpenAI-compatible API.
- **Only the folders you give it.** The folder on show, plus any you add with `/add` or a drop. `/clear` starts over.
- **Cloud and remote folders.** iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, servers and external disks. Plans say when changes sync or reach other people, and nothing is downloaded.
- **One view button.** Grid and List share one button in the header. ⌘1 and ⌘2 still work.

Privacy: Helm connects only to the provider you choose, and only while AI mode is on. It sends your requests and the names and details of files, and short summaries of what's inside them only while Read contents is on. Keys stay in your Mac's keychain. The [README](https://github.com/HuseynAkh/Helm#privacy) has the details.

Helm is an early preview. Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/HuseynAkh/Helm/issues).
