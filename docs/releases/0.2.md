Helm is now an agentic file manager. Ask it to organize, move and find your files. Nothing changes until you press Apply.

- **AI mode**, off until you turn it on. The trefoil at the top right opens the AI panel beside your files, with three suggestions for the folder on show.
- **Plan, then Apply.** Every change shows first, in groups you can untick. A plan can make folders and new files, such as notes, lists, Markdown, CSV, code, RTF or Word, with the text the model wrote. Moving to the Trash, new code and anything that lands where others see it start unticked. One Undo puts everything back, except copies and new folders where there is no Trash.
- **This Folder or Whole Mac.** A switch in the panel lets AI mode look through your home folder, cloud folders and disks, and propose moving files to where they belong. Never your Library, hidden files or system folders.
- **Read contents**, off until you turn it on. The model reads the text of documents, PDFs and Word files, and looks at small copies of pictures, video frames and scanned pages that Helm makes on your Mac without their location or camera details. Never the files themselves.
- **Find with AI.** Describe a file in the search field. The model plans the searches, and Helm runs them on your Mac. A row under the header keeps any search to one kind, and with Read contents the model looks at the best matches, so it finds a photo by what it shows.
- **Setup, step by step.** One window: Welcome, Full Disk Access, Default File Viewer, AI Mode, You're All Set. It carries on where it was after macOS quits and reopens Helm.
- **Your choice of model.** OpenAI with your ChatGPT plan or a key; Claude, Gemini and Grok with your own key; Ollama and LM Studio on your Mac, or any OpenAI-compatible API.
- **Only the folders you give it.** The folder on show, plus any you add with `/add` or a drop. `/clear` starts over.
- **Cloud and remote folders.** iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, servers and external disks. Plans say when changes sync or reach other people, and nothing is downloaded.
- **One view button.** Grid and List share one button in the header. ⌘1 and ⌘2 still work.

Privacy: Helm connects only to the provider you choose, and only while AI mode is on. It sends your requests and the names and details of files. Only while Read contents is on does it send what it reads inside them: the text of documents, and small copies of pictures and video frames. Helm has no servers and collects nothing. Keys stay in your Mac's keychain. The [README](https://github.com/HuseynAkh/Helm#privacy) has the details.

Helm is an early preview. Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/HuseynAkh/Helm/issues).
