The first preview of Helm, a calm, fast file manager for macOS: Finder underneath, Nautilus on top.

- One window with tabs, a path bar you can type into, and a flat sidebar you can arrange.
- Grid and list views. In the list, folders open in place.
- Recent, with folders and files in sections of their own.
- Search in the folder you're in or across the whole Mac.
- Copies that keep dates, extended attributes and permissions. The real Trash, with Put Back. Undo and Redo.
- Quick Look, Open With, Services, and drag and drop that follows Finder's rules.
- A short setup on first launch that explains Full Disk Access.
- Helm can be the app that "Show in Finder" opens, and can sit next to Finder in the Dock.

Helm is an early preview. Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/HuseynAkh/Helm/issues).
