# A plain search kept to one kind. The kind row shows while the search is open, with no spark, at Any. A kind searches
# the words on show again for it alone, and the status bar names it at the end of its line; Any finds every kind
# again. Tab takes the keys to the row, where the arrows choose, and Escape gives them back. Closing the search sets
# the row back to Any; with no words a choice searches nothing, and the next words keep to it. Works in its own tab,
# so the tab that's saved at quit is left as it was.
